01Overview

A full-stack, multi-tenant SaaS platform built for chartered accountancy and audit firms to manage clients, statutory compliance workflows, HR/payroll and firm-wide reporting from one system.

It replaces spreadsheets and email threads with a structured workflow, backed by an immutable audit log and role-based access with granular per-module permissions.

04Key features built

Compliance

Workflow engine

GST, TDS and PT filing tracked through a shared progress-stage pipeline (document checklist, calculation, payment, filing, fee received), generalized across all workflow types.

GST

GST reconciliation

Parses GSTR-2B and purchase-register Excel workbooks, cross-matches invoices and generates Excel/PDF reconciliation reports.

Clients

Client management

Statutory registration tracking (GST, PT, ESI, PF, labour license, shops & establishment) with validity-date monitoring.

HR & payroll

HR & payroll suite

Employee profiles, leave management, itemized reimbursements with multi-bill uploads, geofenced self check-in attendance, payslips and Form 22 muster roll.

Attendance

Single day-mark calendar

A month-view calendar resolves holidays, weekly-offs, approved leave and attendance into one day-mark engine shared across payroll and reporting, so no two views can disagree.

Audit

Immutable audit log

Append-only and SHA-256 hash-chained for tamper detection, with full-text search/filtering and CSV/XLSX/PDF/JSON export.

Reporting

Reports & analytics

Firm-wide compliance status, assignee workload breakdowns and visual charts.

Access

Role-based access control

Granular permission flags per module (HR access, reimbursement approval, attendance approval) layered on top of organisation-scoped roles.
