WRV Energy: Multi-Asset IoT Monitoring Platform

A platform that reads utility bills and equipment displays with OCR and an LLM, matches each scan to the right registered machine, and returns structured readings for human review. Includes the company's live website at wrvenergy.com.

wrvenergy.comFastAPI - PythonNext.js 14Expo / React NativeCloud Vision + Llama 3.3Website live

01Overview

WRV Energy Systems Private Limited is an energy technology and supply chain partner based in Bengaluru, India. The platform lets organisations scan a document or equipment reading (an electricity bill, a UPS status panel, an HVAC display) and get structured data back automatically.

The problem: readings for meters, UPS units, HVAC and other equipment arrive as bills and display photos. The platform identifies which registered machine a scan belongs to from its printed serial, account or meter number, extracts fields with that machine's own template, and has a person review every value before it is saved.

04Platform features

Scanning

Document & reading scans

Single scan or a batch of up to 10 files (PDF and images). PDFs are rendered at about 300 DPI, read with OCR, then parsed into fields.

Matching

Automatic machine matching

Compares printed serial, account or meter numbers with registered machines. If a scan matches none or several, the user chooses.

Templates

Per-machine templates

Reusable field schemas per machine model, with a chosen identifier field and optional extraction guidance.

Review

Human review before saving

Every extracted value is editable and confidence-flagged. Missing fields come back empty and flagged, never made up.

Access

Multi-tenant roles

Admin, client admin and technician roles. Each organisation's data is separate, and technicians only see machines they are granted. New logins go through an admin approval request.

Assets

Seven asset classes

Energy Meters, HVAC Systems, UPS, Data Centre, Solar Inverters, DG Sets, and Pumps & Motors.

Analytics

Readings & analytics

Reading history, per-machine trend charts, dashboards and CSV export.

Reliability

Built to fail gracefully

Retries with backoff on temporary OCR and AI failures, upload limits, batch concurrency control and CORS configuration.

Clients

Web, admin and mobile

A web portal and admin console, plus an Expo mobile app for quick scanning in the field.

05Public website (live)

Website

Marketing site

Hero, approach, how it works, about, asset classes, role-based access, contact form and a map card.

Careers

Sheet-driven careers page

Reads live job openings from a public Google Sheet, with no code changes needed to add or close a role.

SEO & pages

Search-ready

Terms, Privacy, a 404 page, robots.txt and a sitemap. Each page has a canonical URL and the site is registered in Google Search Console.

Design

Responsive and accessible motion

Colour-coded sections, a header that highlights the current section on scroll, and animations that respect reduced-motion settings.

Deploy

Static export on Firebase

A build script produces the website-only site, with the app routes switched off by one setting. Includes domain DNS, HTTPS and apex-to-www handling, with Google Workspace email kept intact.
